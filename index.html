Uncle Rashid's seasonThe Flights

Uncle Rashid explainsHow to fly her

  1. Draw her line. Drag from the falcon on your glove across the sand. Let go and she is cast off: she follows the line, and at its end she circles (she waits on).
  2. She climbs as she flies. The longer she is up, the higher she is (the gold ring round her). Height is speed in the stoop.
  3. Stoop. Hold Space, hold the mouse still, or hold STOOP: she folds and dives the way she is heading, and bends a little toward the quarry. She strikes when she reaches the ground — start too far and she lands short; too close and she overshoots. Let go to pull out (she keeps her speed but loses the height).
  4. Change the line in the air. Drag again at any time and she turns onto the new line.
  5. The shamal. When sand starts to stream across the dunes, a gust is coming: it pushes her with the wind.
  6. A mission is three flights. Bind to the lure or the houbara the number of times the card asks.
  7. Thermals (from mission 2): shimmering rings of rising air. End her line inside one and she circles up three times as fast, past her usual ceiling, to 150. In the oasis they also give back her stamina (the green bar), which runs down as she flies.
  8. The steep stoop (from mission 6): tap, then hold (twice quickly). She falls almost straight down: start it right over the quarry. Too quick for a jinking houbara or a doubling hare. A slanting stoop (just hold) carries far.
  9. The tail chase (from mission 11): low (under 35), close behind the quarry and heading at it, hold: she runs it down on the level for a moment.
  10. The quarry thinks. The houbara jinks as she closes; pigeons fly in a flock that confuses her (take the straggler, the bird trailing behind); the hare doubles back under a slanting stoop. From mission 8 they read you: two slanting stoops and they watch for the next; two steep ones and they watch the sky. Mix them.
  11. Two falcons (from mission 12): Hurra flies with yours, waits on the far side and stoops at the quarry. She never binds; she puts it in. Bind within a moment of her stoop for a one-two (a bigger ring).
  12. Stars. ★ the day · ★★ all three bound · ★★★ that plus the card's named moves, nothing lost. They make your rank.
